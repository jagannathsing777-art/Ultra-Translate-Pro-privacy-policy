🔐 Privacy Policy – UltraTranslate Pro Unlimited
Effective Date: Immediate
Last Updated: 2026

UltraTranslate Pro Unlimited (“we”, “our”, “the App”) is built with a privacy-first, zero-tracking architecture. We respect user confidentiality at the highest professional and ethical level.
This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use our application.

📌 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
UltraTranslate Pro Unlimited does not collect, store, track, or transmit:
❌ Personal information (name, email, phone number)
❌ Login credentials
❌ Location data
❌ Contacts or device identifiers
❌ Usage analytics or behavioral data
❌ Background activity data
There is no user account system, and therefore no identity can ever be linked to usage.

🧠 2. How the App Works (Local-Only Processing)
All core operations are performed locally on your device:
Text input remains on your device
OCR (image to text) is processed locally using on-device computation
Translation requests are sent only for the selected text, without any personal identifiers
We do not log, store, or monitor translated content.

🌐 3. Third-Party Services (Minimal & Controlled)
The App uses limited third-party services strictly for functionality:
Translation API: Used only to convert text between languages
OCR Engine: Runs directly on your device, no image storage
No third-party service receives personal user identity, device data, or history.

🗂️ 4. Local Storage (User-Controlled)
Translation history is stored only on your device
Stored using browser/local app storage
Users can delete history anytime using “Clear All History”
We have no remote access to this data.

🛑 5. Data Sharing Policy
We follow a Zero Data Sharing Policy.
❌ No data selling
❌ No advertising trackers
❌ No analytics SDKs
❌ No background data sync
Your data stays your data.

🔒 6. Security Philosophy
UltraTranslate Pro Unlimited follows modern security principles:
No central server = no mass data breach risk
No accounts = no credential leaks
No background permissions
This architecture dramatically reduces security threats.

👶 7. Children’s Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
Since no personal data is collected at all, compliance is inherently ensured.

🔄 8. Policy Updates
This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect improvements or legal requirements.
Updates will always maintain or improve user privacy protection.

📩 9. Contact
For privacy-related questions or concerns, users may contact the app publisher through the official app listing platform.
